Who We Are

Pawsitive Pet Academy is a pet-care education and practical animal-training business initiated by Sarah Smith.

Our proposed academy is designed for pet owners, animal lovers and people interested in learning about the pet-care industry.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help people understand their pets and build positive relationships through education, patience and practical training.

Our Vision

We aim to promote responsible pet ownership and encourage a better understanding of animal care and training.

Our Values

  • Care and respect for animals
  • Patience and positive learning
  • Trust and professionalism
  • Responsible pet ownership
  • Clear communication with pet owners
