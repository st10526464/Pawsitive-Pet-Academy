Find a Programme for Your Pet

Pawsitive Pet Academy provides information and training opportunities for pet owners who want to improve their understanding of animal care.

1. Obedience Classes

Obedience classes introduce dogs and their owners to basic commands, attention and everyday manners.

Example fee: R350

Suitable for: Owners who want to practise basic commands with their dogs.

2. Advanced Training

Advanced training provides opportunities to practise more challenging skills after basic commands have been introduced.

Example fee: R550

Suitable for: Dogs and owners ready to practise more advanced skills.

3. Behaviour Counselling

Behaviour counselling provides guidance to owners who have questions or concerns about their pets' behaviour.

Example fee: R450

Suitable for: Owners seeking guidance about their pets' behaviour.

4. Pet-Care Education

Pet-care education introduces responsible pet ownership, daily care, safety and animal wellbeing.

Example fee: R300

Suitable for: Pet owners and people interested in learning about animal care.
